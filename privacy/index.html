Privacy Policy — Steamy Dumplings

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Hawkins Crafting Co. ("we") built Steamy Dumplings as a free game. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app does and does not handle.

The short version

Steamy Dumplings collects no personal data. Everything stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app:

  • Requires no account and no sign-in
  • Has no analytics — we don't track how you play
  • Shows no ads and uses no advertising SDKs
  • Sends no data to any server — there is no server

What stays on your device

Your game progress (your dumpling collection, steams, coins, settings, and achievements) is saved in your device's local storage only. It never leaves your phone or tablet. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone — children included — there is nothing to share, sell, or disclose. Parents: there is no chat, no social features, and no way for strangers to contact your child through this app.

Third-party services

Steamy Dumplings uses no third-party SDKs that collect data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
